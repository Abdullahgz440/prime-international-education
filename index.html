Welcome to Prime

Your trusted study-abroad partner.

We help ambitious students make informed choices about studying in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and Cyprus.

Studying abroad can transform your academic, personal and professional future. Prime International Education provides practical counselling, course and university guidance, application support, visa preparation and pre-departure advice.

We listen to your goals, explain realistic options and help you move forward with confidence. We do not guarantee admission or visas—we help you prepare responsibly.
